What Guardian reads and keeps

In plain words. If anything here changes, Guardian will tell you first.

What it reads

Your new email, if you connect Gmail. Read-only: Guardian cannot send, delete, move or label your mail. It looks at the last two days of new mail every few minutes.

Nothing else yet. Calls, texts and card charges are not connected. The sample data you see before connecting Gmail is made up.

What it keeps, and for how long

Message text: 30 days, then deleted. It is stored encrypted, with a key that is yours alone. It is only decrypted for the few seconds a check takes.

What it decided: until you delete your data. For example "phishing email from X, because the link goes to Y". This is how Guardian never alerts you twice about the same thing.

Check history: 30 days. Each step Guardian took and why, so you can always see its reasons.

Who else sees anything

The person you trust, only if they said YES, and only about a serious scam, in one short message.

Superlinked (an AI model) may read a message from a sender you don't know, to judge whether it's a scam. It can only make Guardian more careful, never less.

Persona checks a real person is behind Guardian before it may lock a card. Guardian only learns "passed" or "failed", never your ID or face.

Usage analytics (Amplitude) see counts and outcomes, like "1 alert sent". Never a message, a name, a phone number or an email address.

No one at Guardian reads your messages. Guardian never sells or shares your data.

Your controls

Pause stops every action. You still get alerts.

Disconnect Gmail cancels Guardian's access with Google and deletes your cached mail.

Delete my data destroys your key first, so nothing left anywhere can be read, then removes everything and cancels Google access. It cannot be undone.
